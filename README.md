# Tiny Town Builder

A 3D town building game for kids aged 5 to 13. Build homes, a castle, a school, a hospital, shops, roads, cars and people on a floating island, then tap things to watch them come alive.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install.

## How to play

- Pick something from the tray at the bottom, then tap the grass to build it.
- Drag with the road tool to draw roads. Cars and buses need roads to drive on.
- Tap a building to play with it: the school bell rings, the hospital sends out an ambulance, the duck jumps.
- The card that opens lets you **Move**, **Turn** or **Remove** a building.
- Drag to spin the town, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to move around.
- Press 🌙 for night time.

Keyboard: `R` turn, `Q` / `E` spin, `+` / `-` zoom, `N` day/night, `Esc` close.

## Privacy

The game has no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Towns are saved only in the browser on the device being used.
