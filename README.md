# Tiny Town Builder

A 3D town building game for kids aged 4 to 10. Build homes, a castle, a school, a hospital, shops, roads, cars and people on a big island in the sea, with a mountain, a lake, and a river that runs out across a sandy beach past a little harbour full of boats, then tap things to watch them come alive.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install.

## How to play

- The first time, pick your age (4 to 10). Ages 4–7 are **Little Builders**: fewer, bigger buttons with the most familiar things, roads that get zebra crossings on their own, a simpler card with four paint colours, and tips read out loud. Ages 8–10 are **Big Builders** and get everything. Change it any time with the age button on the right (under ⋯ on phones). The town stays the same either way.
- Tap 🔨 **Build** at the bottom to open the tray, pick something, then tap the grass to build it. Tap ✕ **Close** to tuck the tray away and see the whole town.
- Drag with the road tool to draw roads. Cars and buses need roads to drive on. Draw a road across the river to build a bridge.
- People and pets walk on the grass and pavements, not the road. Put a 🦓 zebra crossing on a road so they can cross: they wait at the kerb, and cars stop for them. Traffic lights can go right on a road too.
- Tap a building to play with it. Homes can be painted 🎨, grown ⭐ (a mailbox, a swing, then a flag and balloons) and named ✏️. 🔄 changes a thing into another of its kind, like a house into a cottage or a tree into a pine.
- Tap a person, pet or vehicle for its own card. Give pets and people names (🎲 picks one for you), and give a pet a ❤️ best friend to walk with.
- Hold your finger on a person or pet to pick them up, then drag to carry them somewhere new. Put them back where they were and they do a trick. Hold a car or bus to ride along.
- The zoo has a giraffe nibbling an acacia tree, an elephant with a pool, a lion on his rock, penguins and a monkey swinging in the tree. The farm has a barn, silo, wheat and lettuce fields, a wind pump, a hen house with pecking hens, and a paddock with a cow, a horse and sheep. Tap them and an animal answers and moves.
- Farm animals come in different breeds (Holstein, Jersey and Highland cows; bay, palomino, grey and black horses; black-faced and black sheep; hens and a rooster; lop-eared rabbits; mallard ducks) and walk on moving legs.
- Every animal has its own voice, made like a real one (a throat, a mouth that opens and closes, breath and a little echo), and no two calls are quite the same: dogs bark, cats meow and purr, cows moo, sheep baa, horses whinny, ducks quack (and their babies peep), and at the zoo, lions roar and elephants trumpet.
- The beach is alive: waves wash up the sand and slide back, crabs scuttle sideways, a kite flies, a speedboat zooms about with a white wake, dolphins jump, and now and then a whale comes up to blow. Tap them to hear them.
- There are little secrets to find: tap the castle at night, plant flowers next to a duck pond and wait, or put street lamps in a row and tap one.
- In the daytime, people go about their day: kids go to school and the playground, and grown-ups go to the shops and the café. At night they go home.
- ↩️ undoes the last change and says what it put back; ↪️ does it again (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y). 🌧️ changes the weather (rain brings umbrellas and puddles, then a rainbow; snow turns the ground white), and 🎵 plays soft music.
- Tap a building to play with it: the school bell rings, the hospital sends out an ambulance, the duck jumps.
- There's lots more to build: a bakery, café, library, police station, train station, zoo and farm; a playground, big wheel, merry-go-round and football pitch; apple trees, a veggie patch that grows, butterflies and a snowman; a lighthouse, sandcastles and beach huts; traffic lights that stop the cars, a bus stop the bus waits at, and cows, sheep, horses, chickens, rabbits and ducks.
- Every home has its own family. They come out to play, wave when you tap them, and walk home and go inside when it gets dark. Knock on a house to call someone to the door.
- The card that opens lets you **Move**, **Turn** or **Remove** a building.
- Drag to move around the island, pinch to zoom, twist two fingers (or right-drag) to spin. On a laptop trackpad, swipe with two fingers to move and pinch to zoom; a mouse wheel zooms. 🎯 takes you back to the village.
- Press 🌙 for night time: the windows light up and everyone goes home to bed.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, `R` turn, `Q` / `E` spin, `+` / `-` zoom, `H` back to the village, `N` day/night, `B` open or close the build tray, `Esc` close.

## Privacy

The game has no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Towns are saved only in the browser on the device being used.
